Where things stand

Omramo is in development and is not yet available. Nothing on this page is an offer, and nothing can be bought yet.

The free sample

Anyone will be able to browse every cover and read the first two pages of any story without paying.

The family subscription

  • One plan. A single auto-renewing family subscription is planned, unlocking the whole library across the Low, Medium and Advanced levels. No level is sold separately.
  • Family Sharing. The subscription is planned to work with Apple’s Family Sharing.
  • Price to be announced. No price has been set. The app will show the price in your local currency before you confirm.
  • Billing through Apple. Payment would be charged to your Apple Account, and the subscription would renew automatically until it is cancelled in Settings.
  • Restore. A Restore Purchases option is planned for families who reinstall the app or change device.

Buying is for grown-ups

A child is never shown a price or a buy button. The subscription screen, the parent view and every link out of the app sit behind a grown-up gate.

The stories

Stories are planned as Omramo’s own retellings of traditional tales with documented public-domain sources, or as original stories written by Omramo and labelled as such. Each story records where it comes from.
