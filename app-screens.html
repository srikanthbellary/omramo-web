Screens · iPhone Duo

Five moments, one fold.

The reader drawn on the device it is for. Closed, the cover screen carries the closed mark. Open, the crease becomes the gutter and every screen is a spread.

Frames are mock-ups, not screenshots. Titles and sample text are in English here; in the reader the stories are Telugu. There is one saffron button in the whole app, and it is on the paywall.

00 Closed 01 Splash 02 Library 03 The spread 04 Paywall

00

Closed

Before anything opens, the phone is a small hardcover. The cover screen shows the six letters on one baseline and the one fact that matters today: which chapter arrived.

  • No status chrome is drawn. The device draws its own.
  • The closed mark is an SVG file, not text, so it never reflows.

01

Splash

Open the phone and the mark opens with it. OM lands on the left pane, MO on the right, and RA sits exactly where the glass folds. The spine of the mark is the spine of the device.

  • Paper, ink, nothing else. The splash is on screen for under a second.
  • The crease is drawn as a soft shadow, the way paper actually curves into a gutter.

02

Library

The shelf on the left, the serial you were reading on the right. Progress is a hairline, not a percentage. The current serial is underlined in ink, because saffron is saved for one place.

  • Rows are 44 points tall or more. Thumbnails are the cover drawings at one weight of ink.
  • Continue is a text link. Nothing on this screen is urgent enough to be a button.

03

The spread

Illustration left, words right. A page turn moves both panes together, so the picture and the paragraph it belongs to never separate. Page numbers sit in the corners where a book keeps them.

  • The drop cap is the only display type on the page.
  • No progress bar, no scrubber. You know where you are because you can see the pages.

04

Paywall

The first chapter is free and the second begins on the left, faded, so you can see that it exists. The card on the right says what you get, then offers the one saffron button in the app.

  • Ink text on saffron, never white. Restore is a plain link beneath it.
  • No countdown, no badge, no strikethrough price. A book does not haggle.
