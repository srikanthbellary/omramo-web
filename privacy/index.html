Privacy

A plain-language summary of the principles the app is being built on. The real policy will replace this page before launch.

DRAFT — not legal text, not yet reviewed

This page summarises intended principles only. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and creates no rights or obligations.

The short version

Omramo is designed to collect nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code in the app.

What the app is designed not to do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in and no Omramo account. No child profile leaves the device.
  • No ads. The app shows no advertising of any kind.
  • No tracking or analytics. The app sends no usage data anywhere and creates no advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No third-party code. The app is planned with no third-party software kits inside it.
  • No notifications aimed at children. The first version does not ask for notification permission.

What stays on the device

To work as a reader, the app keeps a small amount of information on the device itself. It is not sent to Omramo or to anyone else.

  • The reading position in each story.
  • A reading log of which pages were read on which day. It holds no times of day and no durations.
  • Which tap-to-see words have been seen, which characters have been met and which stories are finished.
  • Appearance and text-size settings.
  • A local record that the subscription is active, so stories can be read offline.

This information is part of the device’s normal backup, like other app data.

Purchases

The family subscription is planned to be bought through Apple, using your Apple Account. Apple handles the payment. A child is never shown a price or a buy button, and purchases, the parent view and every link out of the app sit behind a grown-up gate.

Children’s privacy

Omramo is made for children, which is why the design position is to collect nothing at all. That is a design position and not legal clearance: a review against children’s privacy rules, including COPPA in the United States, the GDPR in Europe and the UK children’s code, has not yet been carried out.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties: fonts, images and scripts are all served from omramo.com. There are no forms. Like any website, the company that hosts these pages may keep standard server logs.

To do before launch: confirm the hosting provider and what it logs, and replace this draft with a reviewed policy.
