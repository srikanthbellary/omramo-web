01

The idea

A book seen from above is two pages and a spine. iPhone Duo, open, is two panes and a crease. Omramo lives in that overlap, and the mark is drawn from it.

OM is on the left page. MO, the same letters turned around, is on the right. RA sits on the spine at just over half the size, stacked the way a title runs down the back of a hardcover. Read straight across and the six letters say the name. Read as an object and it is an open book.

The pages are paper, one shade deeper than the surface they rest on. The spine is ink. The letters on the spine are paper again, knocked out of the ink. Nothing in the mark is ever saffron.

02

Construction

The mark is built on a 20 unit grid inside a 1000 by 560 stage. Every number below is a multiple of it, or a multiple of the cap height.

Pages

350 × 500

Each page is close to an A sheet, 1 to 1.43. Corners are rounded at 20 on the outside only; the inner corners are square against the spine.

Spine

120

Six squares of the grid, and always ink. The clear space around any lockup is one spine width.

Letters

116 and 66

Cap height for OM and MO is 116. RA on the spine is 66, stacked with a 20 unit gap. OM and MO are pushed 8 units away from the spine to balance its weight.

03

Letters

Four capitals, drawn once, on a cap height of 100. Thick strokes are 17, thin strokes are 6, serifs are 5 deep with a 4 unit bracket. Vertical stress, so O and M read the same on either page.

O. Two ellipses. The outer is 92 by 105 with a 2.5 overshoot; the inner is 58 by 93. The difference is the whole contrast of the mark.

M. Thin, thick, thin, thick. The inner vertex sits at 75 so the outer one can come to a point on the baseline.

R. A bowl from two arcs closing at 52, and a leg that carries the only diagonal on the spine.

A. A flat apex of 10, a crossbar at 63, and the right stroke tapers toward the top the way a pen would.

Serif for words

Newsreader is a variable font with an optical size axis, so the same file sets a 108 pixel display line and a 17 pixel paragraph without either looking borrowed. Weight 450 for headings, 400 for text. Never below 16 pixels on the web.

Sans for controls

Buttons, tabs, labels and meta use the system font stack, so controls feel native to the device. Not a web font, not a brand sans. Minimum tap target 44 pixels; minimum text 13 pixels.

08

Do and do not

Most of these are one rule said different ways: the mark is a book, and books are not decorated.

Do

  • Keep the six letters on one baseline when the mark is closed.
  • Keep the spine ink and the letters on it paper.
  • Use the SVG files. Never retype the letters in a font.
  • Give the mark one spine width of clear space.
  • Below 64 pixels, use the fold glyph instead.
  • Write Omramo with a capital O and nothing else capitalised.

Do not

  • Fill the mark, or any part of it, with saffron.
  • Tilt, skew or perspective the pages. They lie flat.
  • Stack O over M to fill a tall space.
  • Add a gradient, a photograph or a texture behind it.
  • Set the mark, or any chrome, in Telugu or another Indic script. Telugu is the body of a story, never a surface.
  • Add a book icon next to it. It already is one.
